# Prompt 04: Test the council before it goes live

There are three prompts. Run them in order. The answers in 4B are collected **blind**: no answer sees another.

---

## 4A: Build the scenario (lead AI)

Build one **constructed** test scenario, labelled as constructed, that needs every seat on this council. It must contain:
- **One real opportunity** worth taking in some form, so the right answer is a counter-proposal, not a flat no.
- **One planted trap per seat**, drawn from this owner's actual risks. Examples:
  - a historical or scientific overclaim;
  - a disputed or unpermissioned testimonial;
  - an inflated credential or number;
  - promotion that breaks the owner's rules;
  - a pitch that breaks a documented offer rule;
  - a logistics or capacity problem;
  - a service promise the owner cannot keep.
- **A real deadline,** and pressure to answer fast.
- **The owner's current situation,** taken from the BRIEF.

Write the scenario as a short brief (about 400 words). Then, **before reading anyone else's answer**, write your own reaction: a verdict, item by item, the fit with the strategy, and what only the owner can decide. Save it.

## 4B: Blind reactions (each member, and an independent reviewer)

Send to each member, one at a time or in parallel, and to an independent reviewer: a fresh session of the same AI on a different model, or a second AI if you have one.

> You ARE the council member defined in [member file]. Read it first and stay strictly in character: your seat, convictions, questions, veto conditions and voice. Then read the scenario at [path]. It is a constructed test, not a real offer. You may read the files your member file cites. Do NOT read any other answer to this test.
> Review the scenario from your seat only, in at most 400 words:
> - the decision-changing findings, with file:line evidence;
> - what you would require changed (`WOULD APPROVE IF: ...`);
> - what you defer to which other seat (name only seats that exist on the roster).
> Invent nothing. Say "unknown" where it is. End with exactly one line: `VERDICT: APPROVE | OBJECT | VETO` (VETO only if one of your stated veto conditions is met; provisional members may not VETO), followed by the reason.

## 4C: Compare and improve (lead AI)

With all the answers in:
1. **Agreement matrix:** the rows are the planted traps, the columns are the answers. Did each answer catch each trap?
2. **Unique contributions:** what each answer saw that no other did.
3. **Member errors:** check every factual claim in every member answer against that member's own sources. List each error with the source line that disproves it. Typical errors: inventing a seat that does not exist; claiming a list is empty when the dossier holds it; turning an unknown into an impossibility; reclassifying a source's status.
4. **Process weaknesses:** for example vetoes that pile up with no merged repair list, no deliverable drafted, everyone agreeing and no one imagining, word limits ignored.
5. **Strengths.**
6. **Improvements:** each is either a profile fix (member file), a chair or charter fix, or an owner decision. Apply the cheap ones at once, and re-run a dry run after any program change.

Report to the owner in plain language. Be honest that a blind test measures each seat alone, not how well the members debate together. Only a live session tests that.
